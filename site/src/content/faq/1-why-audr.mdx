question: Why AUDR?
---

AUDR is useful anywhere you need a reliable record of what an agent run consumed
and who or what it was associated with.

Wrap your router, emit the records, and it can help you answer questions like:
How much does this agentic feature cost? What does this customer's agent usage
look like, and how much does it cost? What are the unit economics and margins
per customer for my agentic features? Which workflows or models are driving our
costs? Which power users are driving our costs?
