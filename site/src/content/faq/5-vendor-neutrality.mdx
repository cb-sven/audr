question: Chargebee drafted AUDR. What keeps this from becoming a Chargebee-specific format?
---

We built AUDR as an open standard because we don't think the infrastructure for
agent economics should belong to any one company. Agents run across many models,
routers, tools, applications, and rating systems. The record that connects all of
that usage should be a common language that anyone can speak, not a format
designed to pull you toward a particular vendor.

That belief also shapes how we've built AUDR. The spec carries no prices or
rating logic, and the SDK has no concept of plans, invoices, or how a customer
should be charged. It records what happened and who it happened for. What you do
with that data is up to you. Point the records at Chargebee, a competing rating
engine, your own, or a warehouse for analytics, and AUDR works the same way.
